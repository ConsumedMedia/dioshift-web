# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: \[publish date\]**

This policy explains what information Dioshift LLC ("Dioshift," "we," "us") collects through dioshift.com, how we use it, and who we share it with. We don't sell your information.

## 1. Information we collect

**When you sign up for a plan:**

- Business details: business name, website, category and location (city, state and ZIP)
- Contact details: your name, email address and phone number
- Campaign details: your service area, target audience preferences, preferred start date, a link to your commercial (or a note that you need one), and any notes you add
- Your acceptance of our Terms of Service, with the date and time

**When you pay:** Payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your card or bank details directly; we never see or store your full card number. We keep the Stripe customer and subscription IDs linked to your account, along with your plan and billing status.

**When you contact us:** Your name, business name, email, phone number (if given) and message.

**Automatically:** Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We also use IP addresses to block spam and limit repeated form submissions.

## 2. Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising cookies or ad tracking pixels on dioshift.com. We may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Stripe's checkout page uses its own cookies to process payments and prevent fraud. If we add advertising tracking in the future, we'll update this policy first.

## 3. How we use your information

- To set up, run and deliver your streaming TV campaign
- To bill you and manage your plan
- To send you confirmations, billing notices and updates about your campaign
- To reply to your questions and provide support
- To protect the site from spam, fraud and abuse
- To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Advertising partners**, only to place and deliver your ads. We share your business name, website, commercial and target audience. We don't share your personal contact or payment details with them.
- **Service providers** that run parts of our business for us, such as payment processing (Stripe), website hosting, database storage, email delivery and spam protection. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
- **Legal reasons**, if we're required to by law or need to protect our rights, our customers or the public.
- **Business transfers**, if Dioshift is ever merged, sold or reorganized, as part of that transaction.

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

## 5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and campaign information while your plan is active and for as long as needed afterward for billing, tax and legal records. If you start a signup but don't finish payment, we may keep those details to follow up with you, and we delete them within 12 months. Contact form messages are kept as long as needed to respond and follow up.

## 6. How we protect it

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with providers that use industry standard security. Access is limited to people who need it to run your campaign. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

## 7. Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing support@dioshift.com. We may need to keep some records for legal, tax or billing reasons. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your state's privacy laws, and we'll honor valid requests.

## 8. Children

Dioshift is a service for businesses and isn't intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll post the new version here with a new effective date, and we'll email active customers about important changes.
